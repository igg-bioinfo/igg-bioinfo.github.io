Research

Our work spans the intersection of computation and biology, with a focus on pediatric diseases and rare genetic conditions.

🧬

Genomics

Whole-genome and exome sequencing analysis pipelines for variant calling, annotation, and interpretation in rare disease contexts.

🔬

Epigenomics

Chromatin accessibility, histone modifications, and DNA methylation profiling to decode gene regulation and cell identity.

💻

Tool Development

Open-source pipelines and software for reproducible bioinformatic analyses, made available to the community via GitHub.

🏥

Translational Research

Bridging computational findings to clinical applications at Gaslini, one of Italy's leading pediatric hospitals.

Team

A multidisciplinary group of bioinformaticians, biologists, and data scientists.
